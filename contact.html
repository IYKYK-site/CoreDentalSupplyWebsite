SMS Communications and Verbal Consent

Messaging program: Core Dental Supply Customer Care SMS
Originating number: (754) 260-1004

During a telephone call with a participating dental practice, a caller may request information that can also be delivered by text message. Examples include office directions, appointment details, insurance information, provider information, and patient forms.

The verbal opt-in process works as follows:

  1. The caller contacts a participating dental practice by telephone.
  2. The caller requests appointment information, office information, a patient form, directions, or another customer-care item.
  3. The AI receptionist offers to send that specific requested item by SMS and reads the complete disclosure below.
  4. The caller may affirmatively consent or decline. Declining does not affect telephone assistance or dental services.
  5. No SMS is sent unless the caller clearly gives affirmative verbal consent after hearing the disclosure.

Exact verbal disclosure used during the call:

“Core Dental Supply, on behalf of the dental office, can send the information you requested by text from (754) 260-1004. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is optional and is not required to receive dental services or continue this call. Would you like me to send this information by text?”

The SMS is sent only after the caller explicitly answers yes or otherwise clearly agrees to receive the requested text message.

This campaign does not use SMS keywords to obtain initial consent. Words such as START or UNSTOP may restore messaging only after a recipient has previously opted out; they are not the initial opt-in method described above.

The caller may decline the SMS and continue receiving assistance by telephone. SMS consent is optional and is not required to receive telephone assistance.

Messages are customer-care and informational only. Core Dental Supply does not send marketing, advertising, or promotional SMS messages through this program.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out.
Reply HELP for assistance.

SMS Communications

During a telephone conversation, callers may request that certain information be sent by SMS.

Examples include:

  • Office address and directions
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Patient forms
  • Insurance information
  • Doctor biographies
  • Other customer care information requested by the caller

SMS messages are sent only after the caller provides explicit verbal consent during the telephone conversation.

Core Dental Supply does not use SMS messaging for advertising or promotional marketing.

Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.

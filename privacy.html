Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Core Dental Supply develops software and communication tools for dental practices, including AI-powered receptionists, appointment scheduling, patient communications, and related administrative services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you interact with our services.

Information We Collect

Depending on the services being used, we may collect information including:

  • Name
  • Telephone number
  • Date of birth (when required for appointment verification)
  • Appointment information
  • Information voluntarily provided during telephone conversations
  • SMS communication preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use information only for purposes related to providing administrative services to participating dental practices, including:

  • Scheduling appointments
  • Changing appointments
  • Cancelling appointments
  • Sending information requested by the caller
  • Providing office information
  • Improving our services

SMS Communications

During a telephone conversation, callers may request information such as:

  • Office address
  • Directions
  • Doctor biography
  • Insurance information
  • Patient forms
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Other customer care information

SMS messages are sent only after the caller provides explicit verbal consent during the telephone conversation.

Core Dental Supply does not send promotional or marketing text messages. SMS communications are limited to customer care and information specifically requested by the caller.

Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.

SMS Consent and Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and SMS consent are not sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS consent is voluntary. Callers may decline SMS communications and continue receiving assistance by telephone.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only with participating dental practices and service providers as necessary to provide requested services.

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information.

Although we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this website.

Core Dental Supply

Core Dental Supply develops AI-powered communication solutions that help dental practices improve patient service, reduce administrative workload, and provide faster access to care.

Our flagship solution is an intelligent AI receptionist capable of answering calls, scheduling appointments, providing office information, and assisting patients with common administrative questions while maintaining a natural, professional conversation.

AI Receptionist

Our AI receptionist can assist patients with:

  • Scheduling appointments
  • Changing or cancelling appointments
  • Office hours
  • Office address and directions
  • Insurance questions
  • Doctor biographies
  • Patient forms
  • General office information
  • Urgent call routing when appropriate

SMS Patient Communications

During a telephone conversation, callers may request information by text message for their convenience.

Examples include:

  • Office directions
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Doctor biographies
  • Insurance information
  • Patient forms
  • Office contact information

No SMS messages are sent unless the caller explicitly provides verbal consent during the phone conversation.

Core Dental Supply sends SMS messages solely for customer care and information requested by the caller.

Privacy Commitment

We respect patient privacy.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.
